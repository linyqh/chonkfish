# Chonkfish Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Chonkfish is a chat companion app published by Yingquan Lin ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

- There is no account and we run no servers. We never receive your chats, photos or any other data.
- Everything the app keeps is stored on your device.
- To make the pet talk, the app sends your conversation directly from your device to DeepSeek, an AI service, using the API key you provide.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

## Stored on your device

- Chat history, including photos you attach to messages
- What the pet remembers about you, its diary, notes and reminders
- The pet's name, the name it calls you, its growing personality, and your settings
- Your DeepSeek API key, kept in the iOS Keychain on this device only

This data stays in the app's private storage. iOS may include it in your device backups (iCloud or computer), under Apple's backup terms.

## Sent to DeepSeek

The pet's replies are generated by DeepSeek's AI models. Only after you add your API key and agree, the app sends the following straight from your device to DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com):

- The messages you write and the photos you attach
- Context the pet needs to reply: recent chat history, relevant memories, diary entries, notes, its personality, and the names you've set
- When the pet organizes its memories in the background or plans a message to you: excerpts of your conversations
- When the pet searches the web for you: the search query and the pages it opens
- Requests to check your DeepSeek account balance

These requests are billed to and governed by your own DeepSeek account, under DeepSeek's terms of service and privacy policy, which we do not control. DeepSeek is operated by a company in the People's Republic of China and states in its privacy policy that it stores data there. Please read DeepSeek's privacy policy before adding a key. Requests use encrypted HTTPS.

## Camera, photos and notifications

- The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach a photo. Through the system photo picker, the app sees only the photos you pick.
- Notifications for the pet's messages and reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push service is involved.

## About the AI

The pet is a virtual character powered by AI, not a real person. Its replies are generated automatically and may be wrong; they are not professional, medical, legal or financial advice. If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away (for example 988 in the US and Canada; findahelpline.com lists lines worldwide).

## Children

Chonkfish is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly provide it to them.

## Your choices

- Delete single memories, diary entries and notes under What It Remembers.
- Clear the chat history, remove your API key, or erase all app data in Settings.
- Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
- Data already sent to DeepSeek is handled under DeepSeek's policy; contact DeepSeek to exercise your rights over it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on this page, with a new effective date.
